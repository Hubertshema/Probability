Contingency Table Calculator

🎲 Quick Guide

1️⃣ Name Your Variables

Click on the bold headers in the Data Table (like Boy or Math) to type your own custom categories. The entire probability card will instantly rewrite its formulas to match your scenario!

2️⃣ Punch in the Numbers

Enter your raw frequency data into the four white input boxes. The app automatically calculates your rows, columns, and grand total in real-time.

3️⃣ Read the Magic

  • Marginals: The basics. (e.g., Overall chance of picking a Boy?)
  • Joint (∩): The "AND" rule. (e.g., Boy AND takes Math?)
  • Union (∪): The "OR" rule. (e.g., Boy OR takes Math?)
  • Conditional (|): The "GIVEN" rule. (e.g., If they take Math, what's the chance they are a Boy?)
